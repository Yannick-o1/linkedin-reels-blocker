# Privacy policy: Reels Blocker for LinkedIn

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

Reels Blocker for LinkedIn does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension stores

The extension saves two things in your browser's own extension storage:

- **Your settings:** which blocks are switched on. Chrome may sync these across your devices through your Google account if you have Chrome Sync turned on. That sync is handled by Chrome, not by this extension.
- **A counter:** how many items have been blocked, which is shown in the popup. It stays on your device.

Nothing else is stored.

## What the extension reads

The extension runs only on `linkedin.com` pages. It looks at the page's structure in your browser to find reels carousels, the vertical video player and the Video tab, and hides them. None of the page content, browsing activity or account information leaves your browser.

## Network access

The extension makes no network requests. It has no servers, analytics, tracking or third-party code.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue at https://github.com/Yannick-o1/linkedin-reels-blocker/issues.
